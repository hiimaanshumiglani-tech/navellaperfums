The House of Navella™

Born out of an obsession with formulation purity and lasting projection, Navella™ was founded to prove that world-class, macerated luxury perfumery belongs within everyday reach.

Our Heritage & Craft

Operated under Naavya Inovations in Faridabad, Haryana, Navella™ represents modern Indian olfactory craftsmanship. We craft bold, high-concentration Eau De Parfum blends tailored specifically for tropical climates and long workdays.

Every bottle contains carefully sourced aromatic compounds and concentrated perfume oils that undergo extensive maceration, ensuring balanced dry-downs without chemical harshness.

The Olfactory Signature

From the marine freshness of Hydra to the deep woody warmth of White Oud and the sensual floral mystery of Shadow, each composition is engineered with a layered 3-stage pyramid: bright top notes, expressive hearts, and rich, lingering bases.

Our perfumes speak before you do—leaving a memorable, elegant trail that never overwhelms.
