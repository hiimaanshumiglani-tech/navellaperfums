About Navella Perfumes

Navella Perfumes was born from a simple belief: everyone deserves to smell extraordinary. We craft premium, long-lasting fragrances that don't come with luxury price tags.

Our Story

In a world where premium perfumes cost thousands, we asked: why can't quality fragrances be accessible? Navella Perfumes was founded to bridge this gap — creating perfumes that deliver lasting impressions without breaking the bank.

Each Navella fragrance is carefully formulated using quality ingredients to ensure strong projection and long-lasting sillage. Our 50ml Eau De Parfum bottles are designed to be your daily signature — whether for the boardroom, a night out, or everyday elegance.

Our Promise

Premium Quality

Each perfume is an Eau De Parfum concentration — not EDT — for maximum longevity and projection.

Long Lasting

Our fragrances are designed to be long lasting, so you stay memorable throughout the day.

Affordable Luxury

Premium ingredients shouldn't mean premium prices. Luxury should be accessible to all.

Secure Shopping

All orders are fulfilled via Amazon for reliable delivery and buyer protection.
