Guide 01 · Longevity

How to Maximize Your Perfume’s Longevity All Day

Experiencing quick fade by midday? In India's hot and humid weather, the problem is rarely the perfume concentration—it is almost always rapid skin evaporation. Fragrance aroma compounds bind directly to lipids and moisture. Dry skin absorbs the carrier alcohols and allows volatile top notes to dissipate prematurely.

1. Hydrate the Canvas First

Always apply an unscented moisturizer or neutral skin lotion right out of a warm shower before spraying your fragrance. Moisturized skin creates a physical barrier that locks the perfume oils on the surface, releasing notes slowly and evenly across 8+ hours.

2. Dab, Never Rub

Friction creates heat that shears delicate top notes (like bergamot, lychee, and fresh mint), distorting the opening accords into a muted chemical dry-down. Spray cleanly and allow the mist to dry down naturally.

3. Master Strategic Heat Points

  • Base of the throat & collarbones: Body heat naturally pushes the scent upward.
  • Back of the neck: Leaves a balanced, lingering sillage trail as you walk.
  • Inner elbows & wrists: Blood circulation continuously revives the rich heart and base notes.

Guide 02 · Professional Etiquette

Workplace Olfactory Etiquette: The 2-Foot Rule

Your office fragrance speaks before you speak. In modern air-conditioned corporate spaces, sillage behave differently: cold air concentrates notes, while closed rooms make heavy oriental or cloying sweet gourmands overwhelming to colleagues.

The 2-Foot Radius Principle

Your scent should be discovered upon approach, not announced across the room. If a colleague standing two feet away can subtly detect your perfume, you have applied the exact right amount. Two to three sprays are ideal.

Best Workplace Scent Profiles:

  • Crisp Marine & Aquatics: Clean and refreshing scents like Navella Hydra provide an invigorating presence that cuts through heavy daily commutes[cite: 10].
  • Dry Woods & Clean Musk: Sophisticated blends like Navella White Oud or Azure deliver authority and poise without heavy sweetness[cite: 10].
  • Refined Florals: Subtle rose and lily accords like Navella Noor provide soft, approachable elegance[cite: 10].
What to Avoid: Heavy animalic notes or syrupy vanilla accords in enclosed office cubicles. Save rich, dramatic fragrances like Navella Shadow for evenings and weekend events[cite: 10].
Guide 03 · Custom Formulations

Fragrance Layering: Creating Your Signature Accord

Fragrance layering is the practice of combining complementary scents to craft a custom olfactory presence. By pairing contrasting families—such as crisp aquatics over deep woods—you create a multi-dimensional fragrance that shifts gracefully throughout the day.

The Golden Rule: Heaviest Base Goes First

Always spray the denser, woody, resinous fragrance first (like oud or amber), allowing it 30 seconds to settle on the skin. Then apply the lighter floral or citrus mist on top. This prevents the delicate opening accords from being smothered by heavier base fixatives.

Signature Combinations to Try:

  • The Electric Dusk: Spray Navella Ignite (cardamom/toffee base)[cite: 8, 10], followed by a top mist of Navella Hydra (crisp mint and marine)[cite: 8, 10]. The contrast between cool sea breeze and warm spice is magnetic.
  • The Regal Velvet: Layer Navella Elixir (creamy Turkish rose)[cite: 8, 10] directly over Navella White Oud (smooth cedar and clean musk)[cite: 8, 10] for timeless luxury.
Keep It Simple: Stick to pairing just two fragrances. Layering more than two can create a muddled dry-down that loses the individual character of each note.
